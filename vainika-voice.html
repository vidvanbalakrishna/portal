On Music and Its Making

Articles and spoken reflections by D. Balakrishna

In these pages, D. Balakrishna speaks and writes from inside the tradition — not as a commentator, but as a musician who has spent a lifetime in the company of the veena and its lineage. What follows are articles, spoken reflections, and conversations on music, aesthetics, and the nature of the Mysore Bāṇi.
